About This Site

This site is meant for anyone curious about Judaism — people exploring conversion, people raised without much Jewish education, or anyone who wants their day anchored to the rhythm of the Jewish calendar rather than the secular one. It draws on Hebcal for accurate Hebrew dates and holidays, and Sefaria for Torah text.

Everything here is meant as an introduction and a source of inspiration, not a final halachic ruling — Jewish law has real nuance and depends on personal circumstances and community custom. For anything you plan to actually put into practice, please find a rabbi or teacher to learn with.
