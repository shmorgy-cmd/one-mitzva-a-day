About This Site

This site is meant for anyone curious about Judaism — people exploring conversion, people raised without much Jewish education, or anyone who wants their day anchored to the rhythm of the Jewish calendar rather than the secular one. It draws on Hebcal for accurate Hebrew dates and holidays, and Sefaria for Torah text.

Everything here is meant as an introduction and a source of inspiration, not a final halachic ruling — Jewish law has real nuance and depends on personal circumstances and community custom. For anything you plan to actually put into practice, please find a rabbi or teacher to learn with.

Your privacy

There are no accounts and no cookies. Your settings (city, calendar, profile, reminders) are saved only in this browser. To show dates, times and texts, the page asks Hebcal and Sefaria for data — your saved city goes to Hebcal to calculate times — and city searches go to Open-Meteo. If you turn on weekly & holiday messages, OneSignal stores a subscription ID for this device (no name or email). If you sign up for the weekly email, your email address (and first name, if you give it) is stored by Kit, our email provider, only to send that newsletter. We may count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data.
