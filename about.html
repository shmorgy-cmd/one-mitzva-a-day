About One Mitzva a Day

Why this site exists, who it's for, and how it treats your privacy.

Why I made this

I converted to Judaism. One of my biggest struggles afterwards was simply knowing what to do each day — which prayers, which blessings, what the day on the Jewish calendar was asking of me — and still finding a moment to learn something new and to sit with a spiritual thought.

Shiurim (Torah classes) aren't always easy to get to. And it isn't always comfortable to keep asking questions, again and again.

So I created this guide: one place that shows what today holds, explains it gently, and gives you something to reflect on. My hope is that it helps all of us grow in our journey in Judaism, one mitzvah at a time — and bring a little more light into the world.

Who it's for

Anyone curious about Judaism — people exploring conversion, people raised without much Jewish education, Jews reconnecting, or anyone who wants their day anchored to the rhythm of the Jewish calendar. It draws on Hebcal for accurate Hebrew dates, holidays and times, and on Sefaria for Torah text.

Learn with a rabbi

Everything here is meant as an introduction and a source of inspiration, not a final halachic ruling — Jewish law has real nuance and depends on personal circumstances and community custom. For anything you plan to actually put into practice, please find a rabbi or teacher to learn with. If you spot something that looks wrong, every card has a "Report an error" link.

Your privacy

There are no accounts and no cookies. Your settings (city, calendar, profile, reminders, and the mitzvot you mark as learning or doing) are saved only in this browser. To show dates, times and texts, the page asks Hebcal and Sefaria for data — your saved city goes to Hebcal to calculate times — and city searches go to Open-Meteo. If you turn on weekly & holiday messages, OneSignal stores a subscription ID for this device (no name or email). If you sign up for the weekly email, your email address (and first name, if you give it) is stored by Kit, our email provider, only to send that newsletter. We may count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data.
